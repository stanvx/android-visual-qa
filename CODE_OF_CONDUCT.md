# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported by contacting the project team at
`maintainers@example.invalid`. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and
appropriate to the circumstances.
